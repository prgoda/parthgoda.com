## What Was Actually Agreed

On November 10, at Adobe MAX, [Adobe and Google announced](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-11-10-Adobe-and-Google-Deepen-Partnership-to-Bring-Leading-AI-Models-and-Integrated-Workflows-to-Creatives-and-Enterprises) an expanded version of a partnership that, until now, mostly ran one direction. Google's Gemini, Veo, and Imagen models are going deeper into Firefly, Photoshop, Premiere, and Adobe Express, and Adobe will work directly with Google DeepMind on new image-editing capabilities inside Photoshop. What's new is the reverse flow: Adobe Acrobat is coming to Google Drive and Gmail, Adobe's creative tools are heading into Gemini and AI Mode in Google Search, and Adobe's CX Enterprise Coworker agent will run inside Gemini Enterprise. Business customers also get Adobe Firefly Foundry, which lets them fine-tune Google's models on their own data while running on Google's Vertex AI. "Our expanded partnership with Adobe builds on our commitment to open innovation and choice, bringing together Google's foundation models and Workspace collaboration services with Adobe's creative and productivity tools," [said Thomas Kurian](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-11-10-Adobe-and-Google-Deepen-Partnership-to-Bring-Leading-AI-Models-and-Integrated-Workflows-to-Creatives-and-Enterprises), CEO of Google Cloud. Neither company disclosed pricing, revenue share, exclusivity, or a timeline for most of the new integrations — several outlets, including Morningstar, noted the financial terms simply weren't released.

Adobe's stock has fallen sharply through 2026, a decline commentators have tied to investor worry that generative tools — including Google's own free-standing image and video generators — let people skip Creative Cloud entirely. Rather than build a frontier model to compete, Adobe has bet on being the layer that works with everyone's models and distributes through everyone's surfaces. Reading this expansion as Adobe trading model independence for distribution reach is an interpretation, not something Adobe has stated, but it fits the pattern: Adobe gets Firefly and Acrobat in front of Google's enormous Workspace and Search user base, at the moment it most needs new growth to show investors.

## Who It Affects

Canva and Figma, which compete for the same casual-to-prosumer creative traffic, now face an incumbent with a second major distribution channel through Google Search and Workspace. Microsoft, which bundles Designer and Copilot across its own productivity suite, loses a little of its edge as the "AI is already inside your office apps" argument now applies to Adobe too. For enterprise buyers, Firefly Foundry on Vertex AI is a direct pitch against building custom generative pipelines in-house.

## What to Watch Next

The signals that separate this from a press release: whether Adobe discloses any revenue tied to the Google traffic in a future earnings call, whether the DeepMind-built Photoshop features actually ship rather than staying a roadmap item, and whether Adobe strikes an equivalent two-way deal with OpenAI or Microsoft — which would suggest a genuine multi-model strategy rather than a Google-specific one.
