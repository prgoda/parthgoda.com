## What Was Actually Agreed

On November 23, [Audi](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/audi-integrates-new-app-with-ai-assistant-for-mobile-work-in-the-car-with-cerence-ai-and-microsoft-18450) and [Cerence AI](https://investors.cerence.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/387/audi-debuts-cerence-ais-mobile-work-agent-bringing-enterprise-trusted-access-to-microsoft-365-to-the-car) launched "connected work," an app that lets drivers use Outlook and Teams by voice — checking email, reviewing a calendar, joining a meeting — through an LLM-based agent Cerence built with Microsoft. The car connects as a managed device via Microsoft Intune, Entra, and Purview, and the agent runs on Microsoft Foundry. It ships on the A5, Q5, A6, A6 e-tron, Q6 e-tron, Q7, and Q9 (model year 2026 on), in 28 European countries, in German and English, sold through Audi's MMI experience pro package or its Function Store after delivery. Neither company disclosed pricing.

Cerence calls Audi "the first automaker to jointly develop" this agent with it and Microsoft — a meaningful qualifier, because the underlying concept isn't new. Cerence and Microsoft [unveiled it at IAA Mobility in Munich in September 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/09/08/3145805/0/en/Cerence-AI-Develops-Mobile-Work-AI-Agent-Integrating-Microsoft-365-Copilot-Into-Cars-to-Enable-Safer-Smarter-Work-on-the-Go.html), pitched then as an architecture "any automaker" could use. Audi is the first to actually put it in a shipping car.

As the timeline shows, Audi's parent Volkswagen Group was already a Cerence customer: Cerence's Chat Pro has routed ChatGPT queries through VW's "IDA" assistant [since CES 2024](https://investors.cerence.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/143/volkswagen-and-cerence-collaborate-to-bring-new-generative-ai-powered-interaction-to-drivers), across Volkswagen, Audi, Cupra, Seat, and Škoda. Extending that relationship to Microsoft 365 was a smaller step for Audi than building the integration itself. Rival groups made a different choice: Mercedes-Benz [integrated Microsoft Intune and Teams directly into its MB.OS firmware](https://www.just-auto.com/newsletters/mercedes-benz-brings-microsoft-teams-calls-to-cars) in mid-2025 (with 365 Copilot described then as still "planned"), and BMW [paired directly with Amazon's Alexa+](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/usa/article/detail/T0454787EN_US/a-milestone-for-human-vehicle-interaction-bmw-intelligent-personal-assistant-expanded-to-include) for the iX3 at CES 2026. GM went straight to Google, [rolling Gemini into roughly four million US vehicles](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/22/gm-is-bringing-google-gemini-powered-ai-assistant-to-cars-in-2026) starting in 2026. Reading this as two competing bets — a shared specialist layer versus a direct, deeper tie to one hyperscaler — is an interpretation, not something any company stated, but the pattern across four car groups supports it.

## What Each Side Gets, and What's at Stake

Audi gets a fleet- and business-buyer differentiator — "car as mobile office" — without building AI orchestration in-house. Microsoft gets its security stack (Intune, Entra, Purview) and 365 ecosystem extended into another vehicle brand, reinforcing the enterprise story it's also selling directly to Mercedes. Cerence gets the harder-won thing: proof that automakers still need it as the layer connecting a car to whichever AI backend, rather than going straight to a hyperscaler the way Mercedes, BMW, and GM did. For a 20-plus-year specialist voice-AI vendor increasingly flanked by Big Tech building car-specific products itself, that's closer to a survival signal than a product launch.

## Who It Affects, and What to Watch

Watch whether Volkswagen, Cupra, Seat, or Škoda follow Audi onto the same Microsoft agent, confirming Cerence's shared-layer model scales across brands rather than being an Audi one-off. Watch whether Mercedes actually ships 365 Copilot, closing the gap with Audi's agent without Cerence in the loop at all. And watch pricing, once disclosed, as the signal for how seriously Audi expects fleet and business buyers to pay for it.
